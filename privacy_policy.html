Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Mega Hard handles information when you visit our website or use our projects and services.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Mega Hard is an independent development studio focused on software, games, and technology projects. We aim to respect visitors' privacy and handle information responsibly.

This policy describes our practices for the Mega Hard website and any covered services that link to this policy. Individual projects may have additional privacy notices where necessary.

2. Information We Collect

The information handled by Mega Hard depends on which website features or services you use.

Information You Provide

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Technical Information

When you visit a website, the hosting provider or other technical services may process information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and server logs.

Analytics Information

Mega Hard may use website analytics tools to understand website traffic and improve its pages. These tools may process usage information and identifiers according to their own configurations and policies.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser. Some third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide functionality, measure traffic, or understand website usage.

The use of these technologies depends on which services are enabled on the website. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

4. How We Use Information

Where applicable, information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Operating and maintaining our website and projects.
  • Understanding website traffic and improving usability.
  • Responding to enquiries and feedback.
  • Detecting abuse, investigating security issues, and protecting our services.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as part of our intended website practices. However, information may be processed or shared where necessary with:

  • Hosting providers and infrastructure services.
  • Analytics providers, if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Other service providers required to operate a feature you use.
  • Authorities or other parties where required or permitted by applicable law.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

6. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services. We do not control their privacy practices.

Before sharing personal information with an external service, we recommend reviewing its privacy policy and terms.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, operational needs, or applicable legal requirements.

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion.

You can also manage browser cookies through your browser settings and choose whether to provide information when contacting us.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us through an official contact channel listed on our website.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to encourage anyone to share unnecessary personal information. Where additional protections for children are required by applicable law, we aim to comply with those requirements.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us through an official channel so that the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, projects, services, or legal obligations change.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, contact Mega Hard through an official contact channel listed on our website.
