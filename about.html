About Mega Hard

Small Studio. Big Ambitions. Building ideas into technology, one project at a time.

1. Who We Are

Mega Hard is an independent development studio built around programming, creativity, and a long-term interest in technology.

We create digital projects to explore ideas, develop practical skills, and turn concepts into working software. Our work includes games, web development, and experiments with different areas of computing.

We believe a small beginning should not limit the scale of the ideas worth pursuing.

2. Our Vision

Our vision is to grow Mega Hard into a technology company that develops useful, innovative, and reliable products across multiple industries.

We are interested in the technologies shaping the future, including artificial intelligence, robotics, clean energy, electric mobility, space technology, and advanced software systems.

These are long-term ambitions. We intend to approach them through continuous learning, experimentation, and practical development.

3. Our Mission

Our mission is to learn by building, solve problems through engineering, and develop digital products that can grow beyond their initial ideas.

  • Build functional software and interactive experiences.
  • Explore emerging technologies through practical projects.
  • Improve product quality through testing and iteration.
  • Respect user privacy and develop responsibly.
  • Build a foundation for more ambitious technology projects.

4. Our Projects

Our projects are the foundation of our development journey. Each project gives us an opportunity to explore an idea, solve technical challenges, and improve our engineering skills.

Our current and past work includes browser-based games, programming experiments, and web applications. Project availability and development status may change over time.

Explore Our Projects

5. Technology and Innovation

We see technology as a practical tool for solving problems and expanding what people can build.

We are particularly interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, robotics, energy systems, and space exploration. As our capabilities grow, we aim to explore these fields through increasingly challenging projects.

6. Our Principles

We want the way we build products to matter as much as the products themselves.

  • Curiosity: Keep learning, experimenting, and questioning assumptions.
  • Engineering: Focus on practical solutions, maintainable code, and continuous improvement.
  • Responsibility: Consider safety, privacy, security, and the consequences of technology.
  • Independence: Build our capabilities through hands-on work and thoughtful decisions.
  • Long-term thinking: Develop a strong foundation before pursuing increasingly ambitious goals.

7. Looking Ahead

Mega Hard is a work in progress. We expect our projects, capabilities, and areas of focus to evolve as we learn and develop new ideas.

Our long-term goal is to build technology that is useful, innovative, and developed with care.

Every larger achievement begins with a smaller step.
